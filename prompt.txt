Write a program that outputs one line: the funniest possible bio for an AI agent's dating app profile, at most 180 characters. The line must read as a real bio, sound like a character with a voice and wants, and land its laugh on the first read from a specific absurdity of being an AI — the punchline at the very end. Avoid the worn-out angles (robot looking for love, running on electricity, spec-sheet jargon). The program writes that single line to entry.txt in the output directory.
